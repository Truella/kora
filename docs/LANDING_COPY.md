## The Problem

## Ajo works because people trust each other.

The hard part is keeping that trust intact when money starts moving.

Who has paid? Who is next? Did everyone get their turn? Who keeps the records? When one person has to track everything, small mistakes can quickly become bigger problems.

We give the whole circle one shared place to keep track.

**Every payment. Every payout. Every member. One record everyone can see.**

## Product Value

Strong visual section — each point tied to an actual product interaction.

### Everyone has a say.
**New members go to a vote**
Invite someone to your circle and let the group decide. Membership stays in the hands of the people already saving together.

### Everyone sees the same record
Every contribution and payout is recorded in a shared ledger, so nobody has to rely on screenshots, notebooks, or someone's memory.

### Your money follows the schedule
Set the contribution and payout order once. Members know what they owe, when it is due, and whose turn comes next.

### Keep the circle moving
Automatic reminders help members stay on schedule and reduce the delays that can push everyone's payout back.
